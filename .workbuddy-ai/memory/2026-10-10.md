# 2026-10-10

## Redesigned the contact "Before you write" block (rack-01)

`rack-01-module__…__contactBrief` in `features/landing-page/rack-01/section-contact.tsx`
+ `rack-01.module.css`. The old block was flat editorial type on the cream
chassis band — uppercase Syne questions at 26px with a bare mono index and a
plain chevron. It read as a page of copy sitting under a device.

New reading: the deck is the hardware, this block is the printed insert that
ships in the box. Concretely:

- `.contactBriefPanel` — a card, `--chassis-hi` fill, hairline border, 12px
  radius, soft shadow. `max-width: 780px`, which is the deck's own width, so
  the device and its card stack as one centred column.
- `.contactBriefHead` — label left / `04 entries` right, hairline under both:
  the same left/right shape as the deck's `contactDeckBrand` bar.
- Two `Screw` at the top corners (the import in section-contact.tsx was unused
  before this; it now is used). Hidden under 768px.
- Rows are channel strips: 30px boxed mono number, question in Syne sentence
  case at 21px (was uppercase 26px), 28px boxed chevron. Open row lights its
  number with `--signal-orange` and takes `--panel-fill`.
- Answer aligns under the question via `calc(26px + 30px + gap)`; on mobile the
  indent is dropped.

Decisions worth keeping:

- The rack's global focus ring is `.root button:focus-visible` → 3px
  `--signal-orange` at a 4px offset. On a full-bleed row inside an
  `overflow: hidden` card that offset ring is clipped at both ends and reads as
  two rules. Overridden at `.root` specificity with the same colour and weight
  at `outline-offset: -3px`.
- Question type is sentence case because the rack already sets headings and
  card titles in sentence case and the questions are written as sentences.

Shipped as `d91abd8` on `main` (`tweak: rebuild the contact brief as the deck's
insert card`), pushed to `origin/main`. `.workbuddy-ai/` is untracked and stays
that way — it is not part of the repo.

`type: subject` in lowercase imperative (`feat:` / `fix:` / `tweak:`), then a
body of short paragraphs that explain the reasoning and name the measurements,
and a `Co-authored-by: CommandCodeBot <noreply@commandcode.ai>` trailer on
nearly every commit.

## e2e

`e2e/contact-deck.spec.ts` assertions updated for the new geometry (padding
18px/26px, contrast ≥ 4.5:1 computed instead of a channel-sum threshold, plus a
new test that the silkscreen entry count matches the rendered row count).

Known and pre-existing: `accordion opens and closes` fails on `main` too. Root
cause found: the contact section's height only materialises after the
IntersectionObserver-driven layout runs, so `scrollIntoViewIfNeeded` can no-op
while the trigger is still below the document's scrollable extent, and the
click lands nowhere. Not fixed (e2e deprioritised by the user).
