## The hybrid's image story (Tuner + Waveform) — storyboard delivered

User asked specifically how the *image story* works for the hybrid. Six frames,
as fractions of one scroll window:

| Progress | Beat | What moves |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | Dead air | static fills the dial band, needle parked left, readout NO SIGNAL. **The name is absent** — that is the hook |
| 0.20 | Sweeping | needle travels, frequency counts up, static thins, name ghosts through misaligned at low opacity. Static amplitude tracks scroll velocity |
| 0.35 | First catch | needle decelerates, left half of the waveform resolves, right half still static, signal bar at a third |
| 0.55 | Locked on | static gone, waveform clean, bar full, readout LOCKED. Title at max clarity — and it *is* the waveform |
| 0.70 | Playing | needle becomes a playhead reading across the wave; bars fill amber behind it. Proof numbers sit as markers it reaches |
| 0.90 | Flattened | amplitude collapses to zero over ~0.12; wave becomes a hairline; playhead parks far right; About's grid ticks grow downward from the hairline |

**Four continuity threads carry the seam** (this is why it reads as one move):

1. waveform bars → About's grid ticks (same pitch)
2. tuning needle → About's index marker 02 (one vertical line)
3. frequency readout → About's section number
4. the name → About's silkscreen label (same type)

Audio note: the lock-on frame (0.55) is the natural place for a single chord,
but only after user interaction — never autoplay.

### What building it proved — folded into the doc, not lost

The four real bugs found by measuring in a browser are recorded in the doc
(`§1.3` the property host, `§9` the seam fallback, `§12` the two traps). The
findings worth carrying forward:

- **The property host must be the smallest node that reads the values.** Writing
  animated custom properties on `.camera` makes them invisible to the ruler,
  which is the camera's *sibling* (it must not scale). Both the live tick rule
  and the quarter-tick reveal resolve to nothing and the ruler silently prints an
  unzoomed 2018–2026 while the clips are at scale 2. Host is `.trackArea`.
- **Never trigger a ScrollTrigger on a `position: sticky` stage** — its bounding
  rect is scroll-dependent, so `top top` / `bottom bottom` resolve to the same
  offset and the range is zero-length. Trigger the tall section that contains it.
  Corollary: a window opening at scroll 0 never fires `onEnter`; use `onToggle`.
- **The identity-transform fallback is real.** `cameraX = worldWidth/2 - focus*scale`
  is exactly `(0,0)` at the overview, so the CSS property fallbacks *are* the
  settled frame — reduced-motion and no-JS need no separate rendering. Verified:
  under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the camera computes
  `matrix(1, 0, 0, 1, 0, 0)` with **zero GSAP/Lenis requests**.
- **`useRackAnimations`' `if (!hero) return` kills the whole page.** It sits
  inside the `min-width: 769px` callback, so any hero not carrying
  `rack-01.module.css`'s `.hero` class takes the about scrubber, section
  reveals, skills sequence and experience→work seam down with it — silently.
  §11 predicted this; it is the first thing to fix for any new hero.

## Seam rework — restored About, clip-path zoom, the read

Requested: use the **previous** About component and make the transition to About
a timeline **zoom-in** rather than a scroll to a section below.

### What changed

- **`rack-01/section-about.tsx` restored from `HEAD`** and adapted, not
  rewritten: `scrollProgress` dropped, `PARKED_PLAYHEAD = 0.62`, wrapper changed
  from `<section id="about">` to `<div className={about + aboutSeam}>`.
  `arrangement-about.tsx` (the intermediate replacement) is deleted.
- **The reveal is `clip-path: inset(...)`, not `transform: scale()`.** Scaling
  was rejected: the clip is 58px tall at the start of the dive, so a panel scaled
  to fit it renders About's heading at a fraction of its size.
- **Phases retimed** in `arrangement-camera.ts`: `settle 0.1 · travel 0.3 ·
  zoom 0.52 · rail 0.72`, plus a new `ARRANGEMENT_SEAM_OPEN = 0.82`.
- **The hold is split in two.** `rail → seamOpen` opens the window; `seamOpen → 1`
  is **the read**. See below for why.
- `.aboutSeam` in `rack-01.module.css`: `overflow: visible` (the parent's
  clip-path bounds the window), `justify-content: flex-start`, and
  `transform: translateY(var(--arr-seam-scroll))`.
- `.seam` carries `background: var(--chassis-hi)` — the ground must live on the
  *window*, because the read translates the panel and a travelling ground leaves
  a strip of dark arrangement along the bottom edge.

### The read, and why it exists

**About does not fit the window it is revealed in.** Measured overflow of the
panel against the window: **0px @1728×1117, 74px @1512×982, 191px @1440×900,
227px @1280×800, 304px @1280×720.** It is a function of the viewport, so no
constant covers it. `measure()` now reads `seamOverflow` and the frame returns
`seamScroll = readT × seamOverflow`; the hook writes `--arr-seam-scroll` as a
negative px. Verified: at `p=1` the last card's bottom lands flush with the window
(`clearance: 0`) at 1440×900 and 1280×720, and at 1728×1117 nothing translates.

Do **not** merge the two stretches back into one — sliding the panel while it is
still half clipped reads as the layout breaking, not as the page settling.

### Three bugs found while verifying

1. **`inert` was never applied.** `render(0)` ran while `section.dataset.phase`
   was already `"open"` (it ships as an initial JSX attribute), so the change-only
   guard made the first frame a no-op and the clipped About panel stayed in the
   tab order. The hook now tracks the phase it last *applied* in a local starting
   from `null`; `cleanup` clears `inert`, without which a resize across the 768px
   breakpoint leaves the panel keyboard-unreachable.
2. **`inert` cleared too early.** It was tied to `data-phase === 'rail'`, which is
   where the window *starts* opening — so a keyboard user could tab into a clipped
   strip. Reachability is now its own signal: `frame.railReveal >= 1` drives both
   `seam.inert` and a new `data-seam="open|closed"` that gates `pointer-events`.
   Verified: `focus()` on a card button is a no-op while closed, works when open.
3. **`#about` landed on a closed strip.** The anchor was placed at
   `rail × travel`, but `rail` is where the window *begins* opening. It now sits
   at `ARRANGEMENT_SEAM_OPEN × travel`, written in px by the hook from the
   trigger's own range. Verified: after a jump, all four clip-path insets are `0`
   and the heading is inside the visible window.

**Measurement trap:** `getBoundingClientRect()` ignores `clip-path`, so a
bounding-box check passes on a fully closed seam. Check the `--arr-seam-*`
custom properties, or the computed `clip-path`, not the rect.

## Chrome and proof step aside for the panel

Requested: once About is showing, the title bar (`chrome`) and the footer
(`proof`) should be hidden.

### The change

- **`.seam` moved from inside `.trackArea` to a direct child of `.stage`.** This
  is the whole change. `.trackArea` is `overflow: hidden` (the camera's
  transformed lanes must be clipped to it), so a seam inside it could never paint
  over the furniture above and below. As a stage overlay with `z-index: 5` it
  covers both.
- **`ArrangementGeometry` gained `chromeHeight` (42) and `proofHeight` (66).**
  The seam's window is now measured from the *stage's* edges: the frame adds both
  to `clipTop` / `clipBottom` and mixes them away. Without that the window sits
  one chrome-height too high and the dive reveals the wrong part of the panel.
  At `rail` the window is still inside the track area (so the furniture is
  untouched through the dive); by `seamOpen` all four insets are exactly `0`.
- **`data-seam="open"` sets `visibility: hidden`** on `.chrome` and `.proof`.
  They are already painted over by then, so this is not a visual change — it is
  what removes the nav and the "Open profile" link from the tab order. Use
  `visibility`, not `display`: collapsing the chrome would move every lane out
  from under a camera measured once per refresh.

### Four bugs found on the way

1. **The inheritance trap, again.** `--arr-seam-*` were written on `.trackArea`.
   Fine while the seam was its child; as a *sibling* the seam inherits nothing, so
   every inset fell back to `0` and `--arr-seam-opacity` to `0` — a panel fully
   open and completely invisible. Now written on `.seam` itself (also the smallest
   node that reads them). **My first verification missed it because it read the
   properties off `.trackArea` — the source, not the consumer.** Always read a
   custom property off the element that *uses* it.
2. **`.aboutSeam` lost its containing block in the fallbacks.** With the seam
   `position: relative` under reduced motion and with scripting off, an absolute
   `.aboutSeam` has no sized ancestor and contributes no height: measured, the
   **hero collapsed to 108px and the panel to 40px**. The flow rule was
   phone-only; it now fires on all three conditions in one comma-separated query
   (`(max-width: 768px), (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce), (scripting: none)` —
   comma media queries are OR'd). **Both halves of the unpin must agree**: any
   condition added to one list must be added to the other.
3. **A latent 19px camera error.** `measure()` passed
   `viewportHeight = clientHeight - rulerHeight` while `clipBottom` assumed it had
   *not* been subtracted, so the profile clip sat 19px above the band's centre —
   half the ruler. `viewportHeight` now means the track area's full height, as its
   doc comment always said. A test pins the vertical centring.
4. **The seam's `position: static` in fallbacks** would have let an absolute
   `.aboutSeam` resolve against `.hero` and cover the whole section. Now
   `position: relative` in all three fallback blocks.

## 13.7 — The read moves the column, not the panel

Requested: "like prev animation scroll on about section timeline is stay and rail
card is moving to top." The read had been translating the *whole* `.aboutSeam` as
one piece, which slid the timeline along with everything else. The old About was
a page section whose animation was pure CSS: `.timelinePanel { position: sticky;
top: clamp(24px, 7vh, 72px) }` beside a scrolling column of cards. That is the
animation to reproduce.

### What changed

- **`rack-01/section-about.tsx`** — two measurement hooks: `data-about-rail` on
  `.aboutCardRail`, `data-about-read` on `.aboutDeck`.
- **`rack-01/rack-01.module.css`** — dropped `transform: translateY(var(--arr-
  seam-scroll))` from `.aboutSeam`; the transform now sits on
  `.aboutSeam .sectionHeading, .aboutSeam .aboutCardStack` (both, because nothing
  wraps exactly those two — the deck between them also holds the timeline).
  `.aboutSeam .timelinePanel` is now `position: relative`, not `sticky`. The
  fallback block resets `transform: none; will-change: auto`.
- **`use-arrangement-hero.ts`** — `readOverflow` is now
  `max(0, rail.offsetHeight - deck.offsetHeight)`.

### Why `offsetHeight` and not a rect

The stack carries the read's own translate, so a bounding rect measures the moved
element and the travel shrinks by its own displacement on every re-measure.
Layout heights are what the travel has to close; transforms never touch them.

### Why rail-vs-deck is the right pair

The rail's top is flush with the deck's top (the stack is the deck's first grid
item, the rail the stack's only child) and the deck's bottom is the stage's
content bottom (the deck is the `flex: 1` child of a `height: 100%` stage). So
the difference *is* the travel that lands the last card flush. The rail's
`offsetHeight` is `max(content, deck)` because it stretches to fill the stack, so
a roomy viewport reports exactly `0` rather than a negative travel.

### Trap to remember

`.timelinePanel` must stay `position: relative` (not `static`) in every fallback:
its screws are absolutely positioned against it, and dropping it out of the
positioned chain resolves them against an ancestor and slides them off the
chassis.
