## 13.12 — the dock's band is reserved, and the panel gives up the difference

Requested: "can you fix on timeline it collison with navbar bottom maybe on
container section about width its like in experience section", with a screenshot
of the workstation's footer sitting under the quick navigation. Full record in
`docs/arrangement-hero.md` §13.12.

**The width suggestion was a dead end — say so next time rather than trying it.**
The dock is `fixed` (20px off the bottom, 56px tall, so it owns the bottom 76px)
and *centred*, 256px wide. The panel is top-aligned in the deck with a content
height, so its bottom edge is `deckTop + 552.5` at every viewport. Narrowing the
About container to the experience section's `min(1050px, 100%)` moves the panel's
left edge 644 → 679 against a dock at 627…885: still 206px of horizontal overlap,
and the panel gets *taller* because its readout sentence wraps.

**The real bug: nothing in About knew the dock existed.** Measured before the fix
at 1512×784 (the reporter's window — 1920×993 screenshot at 1.27 scale):
panel `172.3 → 724.8` vs dock `708.6 → 764.6`, **16px of overlap across 241px of
the dock's width**, straight across the "CLIP nn / SELECTED" readout. 58px at
1280×720. Nothing above ~800px tall collided, which is why earlier passes at 900
and 982 all passed. **Lesson: when a collision is reported, sweep viewport
*heights*, not just widths — 784 and 720 were never in the set.**

**Fix, two halves.**
1. `padding-bottom: calc(76px + max(0px, 620px - 100svh))` on
   `.aboutSeam .aboutDeck`. Padding and not a shorter seam because the deck is
   `flex: 1` — its border box is the stage's leftover either way, so
   `offsetHeight` does not move, and `offsetHeight` is what the read measures its
   travel from. The travel stays exactly −194px at 1440×900.
2. `max-height: 100%` on the panel (resolves against the grid area = the space
   above the dock) + a flex chain so the difference is *distributed* rather than
   clipped. A `max-height` on a block would just clip, and the first thing off
   the bottom is the readout the rule exists to protect.

**The correction that mattered: `flex: auto` was wrong.** The first pass let the
panel lose 42px at *every* viewport, including 1728×1117 where nothing collided.
An auto-height grid resolves `1fr` rows to max-content, and a lane's max-content
is its content (89px), not the 104px it is drawn at. The basis must be written
out: `flex: 1 1 calc(34px + 3 * 104px)` with `min-height: 0`.

**Below ~780px tall the lanes reach their floor (89px, a track header) and the
third crops mid-clip**, so a `@media (min-width: 769px) and (max-height: 780px)`
block sheds 49px of chrome (brand 58→44, header padding 5px, track header 9px,
readout 8px). Nothing dropped, nothing hidden.

**Two near-misses worth remembering.**
- The phone's `display: none` on the panel is `.timelinePanel` — one class
  *lighter* than the `.aboutSeam .timelinePanel { display: flex }` this added to
  the same box. The seam would have won and the panel would have come back on the
  one viewport it is never drawn on. Needed an explicit `.aboutSeam .timelinePanel
  { display: none }` in the phone query. **Same class as §13.7/§13.10: a
  declaration that is right in one layout state silently out-specifies the rule
  that was handling another.**
- The flow fallbacks (phone / reduced motion / no JS) had to undo the whole
  thing: no deck content box to cap against, and the reserved band would be 76px
  of dead space under the last card.

**Verified** (five progress points per viewport): overlap 0 at 1728×1117,
1512×982, 1440×900, 1512×784, 1280×720; panel keeps 552.5px and 104px lanes at
1440×900 and above (main's height), shrinks to 518.5/92.7 at 784 and 479.1/88.2 at

## About rebuilt — the Patch becomes a descent (The Depth Stack)

Implemented `docs/ABOUT_SCROLL_CONCEPT.md`. The dive is untouched; what is
inside the clip changed.

**Composition.** Three planes, one per `MIXER_DATA` group, all in one grid cell
and told apart by depth rather than by position. Each plane is three layers at
three rates — `.plate` 0.40x (the head, the tech note, the level field),
`.focal` 1.00x (the heading and the story, deliberately with no transform at
all), `.edge` 1.35x (credits, aside, résumé). The dots are buttons that scroll
to a plane rather than only selecting it, because the descent is scroll-driven
and a selection that does not move the scroll lights a dot and changes nothing.

**One number per plane.** The hook writes `--plane-0..2`, a signed depth
(`across - index`). The stylesheet derives the plane's presence from it with a
two-term `min()` window (`--ramp` / `--hold`, no `abs()`), and each layer's
travel from that same number, so the read still costs three writes however many
layers or channels a plane grows.

**The bug nearly shipped: `across` must stop at the last plane's focal point.**
`PATCH_ACROSS_MAX = PATCH_MODULES - 1 + PATCH_FOCAL` = 2.5, not 3. Running to 3
puts the last plane's focal point at 2.5 and then spends the final sixth of the
read fading it out again, so the section ends on an empty panel. Pinned by a
test that asserts the last plane is the active one at the end of the read.

**Fallbacks are per plane, and that is load-bearing.** The inline `--d` fallback
is `PATCH_FOCAL` for plane 0 and `-1` for the others. A shared fallback would put
all three planes at the focal depth at once — three stories stacked on top of
each other in every frame where the hook has not run (no script, reduced
motion).

**Retired:** `Knob`, `Fader` and their CSS; `knobAngle` / `KNOB_MIN` /
`KNOB_MAX`; `PATCH_SWEEP_SHARE`. `LevelBar` replaces them in the kit with the
same contract (`role="img"`, the level in the label, the name inside the
element), filled by `scaleY` rather than a `height` so the only animated
property is a transform. `controlSweep` / `PATCH_STAGGER` / `room` survive —
the level bars still stagger in, and `--ctl` moved onto `.levelBar`.

Gates: tsc clean, eslint clean, jest 200/200 across 12 suites, next build clean.
