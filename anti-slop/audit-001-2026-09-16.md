## Findings

- Rule: motion performance and restraint
- Location: `features/landing-page/rack-01/rack-01.module.css:8699`
- Reason: the handoff cut line permanently reserves a compositor layer. This is a single element, so the impact should be small, but it is still worth validating on lower-powered devices.
