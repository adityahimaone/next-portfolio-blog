About — 02 / the rules become the work

The rules become the work.

I build interfaces where the constraint is the composition. Every element on this page is derived from real data — a clip's width is how much of the craft it covers, a ruler tick is a stage of production, a meter is a live signal. Nothing here is decoration that happens to look technical.

Signal

Interaction is an instrument. Inputs are read, state is audible, and the interface responds on the frame the reader acts — not the frame after.

Systems

Tokens, primitives, and a single motion vocabulary. One number drives a whole surface, so the parts cannot drift apart from each other.

Craft

Motion is spent, never sprayed. Every transition has a reason and a measured cost, and it reverses frame-for-frame on the way back up.

The session folds down to the profile, the profile steps aside — and the work is already there.
